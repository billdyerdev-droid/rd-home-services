Epoxy Flooring FAQs

Where is epoxy flooring commonly installed?

Garages are the most common residential epoxy project, but workshops, storage areas, and some concrete utility spaces may also be a fit.

Is epoxy easier to clean than bare concrete?

Yes. A properly installed epoxy coating can make the surface easier to sweep, wipe, and maintain than unfinished concrete.

Can epoxy improve the look of a garage?

Yes. Epoxy and flake floor systems can give a garage a cleaner, more finished appearance.
