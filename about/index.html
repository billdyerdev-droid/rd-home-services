About RD Home Services

RD Home Services was built around a simple idea: homeowners shouldn't have to manage five different contractors to take care of one property.

We started with roofing, epoxy flooring, and artificial turf — three services that transform the most visible parts of your home. Roofing remains our flagship. Whether you need a repair, a full replacement, storm damage documentation, or help navigating an insurance claim, we handle it with clear communication and no runaround.

As we've grown, so has our scope. We now coordinate trusted local professionals for painting, fencing, siding, and general home repairs — vetting the work, managing the relationship, and making sure the job gets done the way we'd want it done on our own home.

We serve The Woodlands, Magnolia, Conroe, and the surrounding North Houston area. If you're not sure whether we cover your area or handle your project, just call. We'll tell you straight.
