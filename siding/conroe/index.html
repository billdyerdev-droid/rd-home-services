Frequently Asked Questions

Can you match a repair to my existing siding in Conroe?

In most cases, yes. We look at the existing siding's color, profile, and condition before recommending a patch repair versus a broader replacement.

Do you handle storm-damaged siding?

Yes. If storm damage is involved, we can help document it for an insurance claim, similar to how we handle roof storm damage.
